# Contributing

Fork, modify and share New Start for purposes permitted by the non-commercial licence. Keep the licence and required notice with redistributed copies. Contributions must be compatible with those terms; submitting a contribution offers it under the repository's existing licence, without transferring copyright ownership.

Build with `./build.ps1`, explain the user-visible change, and include the relevant validation from [testing](docs/TESTING.md). Keep fixes focused and retain Explorer fallback. Do not introduce taskbar registry writes, process injection, telemetry or taskbar suppression without a separately reviewed design and recovery strategy.

Never submit your settings, app inventory, account image, personal screenshots, tokens, diagnostic dumps or build intermediates. Use synthetic pin paths and generic examples. Report problems with the Windows build number, display scaling, dock edge/alignment and steps to reproduce; redact personal details.
